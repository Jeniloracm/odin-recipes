Lentil Dal

Description

Adaptable recipe that could be made with vege mince or meat

Ingredients

  • 4 oz Red lentils
  • 1 large white onion
  • 4 Cloves of garlic
  • 8 tblsp Olive oil
  • Tin of plum tomatoes
  • Pint of veg stock
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 Black union seeds (nigella)
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Cook lentils, tomatoes and stock on the hob for 30 minutes or until the lentils are soft
  2. I a heavy bottomed pan, toast the seeds ove a low heat for a few mins
  3. Add the oil. when the oil is hotm add chopped onion and garlic and cook on a med heat for 10 minutes
  4. Add the onion mix to the main pan and continue cooking for 10 minutes. Add more water to get the consistency you like and season to taste
